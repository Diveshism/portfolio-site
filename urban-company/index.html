02 · Reading the market

In the UAE, home services run on a calendar, not just a need.

The UAE household is expat-heavy, digitally native, time-poor, and lives through a 45°C summer. That shapes when people book and why — which is where marketing either wins or wastes money.

~88%
of UAE residents are expatriates — households that often arrived without a trusted local network of service pros.
~99%
internet penetration — one of the highest globally. The first touchpoint is almost always a phone.
45°C+
summer peaks. AC servicing isn't a nice-to-have; a failed unit in July is an emergency.
~4M
people in Dubai alone, with constant inflow — every new tenant is a new customer with zero brand loyalty yet.

The new mover

Just signed an Ejari in JVC or Dubai Marina. Needs deep cleaning, pest control, painting, AC check — all in one week. Highest-intent customer in the market, and nobody owns them yet.

The dual-income family

Villa or 3BR in Mirdif or Arabian Ranches. Books cleaning on repeat, salon at home on weekends. Wants consistency and the same trusted professional — a retention play, not an acquisition one.

The summer leaver

Flies home July–August. Worries about the AC, plants, and dust in an empty flat. Under-served: a "home care while you're away" bundle doesn't really exist as a product story.

04 · Ready-to-test ideas

Three campaigns, each tied to a moment that already exists.

Good home-services marketing doesn't invent demand. It shows up, with the right offer, at the exact moment the need appears.

Mar – May · AC

Beat the Heat Before It Beats You

"Your AC has worked hard all winter doing nothing. Give it a check-up before it has to save your summer."
Audience
Apartment renters, last AC service > 6 months
Channels
Google Search, Meta, in-app push
Hook
Book in April, priority slot guaranteed in July
KPI
Pre-season bookings, July repeat rate
Always-on · Movers

Day One, Done.

"New keys. New neighbourhood. Same exhausting to-do list — except now it's one booking."
Audience
New tenants, first 30 days after move-in
Channels
Property-portal & brokerage partnerships, building QR codes
Hook
Move-in bundle: deep clean + pest control + AC
KPI
Bundle attach rate, 90-day retention
Jun – Aug · Summer

Go Home. We'll Mind Yours.

"Ten weeks in Kochi, Manchester or Manila. Come back to a cool, clean home — not a dust storm."
Audience
Expat families travelling for summer
Channels
Instagram Reels, school & community WhatsApp groups, email
Hook
Pre-departure AC service + welcome-back deep clean
KPI
Pre-booked return-cleans, referral shares
05 · The 90-day plan

Three months. Three outcomes you can measure.

An internship is short. Here's how I'd make sure it leaves something behind that the team keeps using.

Learn & audit

Weeks 1–4

  • Shadow performance, brand and CRM teams
  • Audit current funnel and channel reporting
  • Teardown of competitor offers and creatives in the UAE
  • Validate the demand calendar with booking data

Output: a one-page funnel diagnosis with the top 3 leaks.

Build & test

Weeks 5–8

  • Launch 1–2 small, well-scoped campaign tests
  • Write A/B variants for ads, push and email copy
  • Build a weekly channel scorecard (CAC, ROAS, repeat)
  • Interview 10–15 customers on why they rebook

Output: live test results with a clear keep / kill / scale call.

Scale & hand over

Weeks 9–12

  • Double down on the winning test
  • Automate the scorecard in Power BI / Looker Studio
  • Document playbooks for the next seasonal peak
  • Present findings to the marketing lead

Output: a dashboard and playbook the team keeps after I leave.
